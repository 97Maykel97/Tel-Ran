Chicken with Potatoes

In the oven

A simple and delicious dish made with everyday ingredients. Perfect for a family dinner!

INGREDIENTS:

  • 500 g chicken fillet or chicken thighs
  • 700 g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

INSTRUCTIONS:

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut them into large wedges.
  3. Cut the chicken into serving-size pieces.
  4. Mix the vegetable oil, minced garlic, paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Add the marinade to the chicken and potatoes, then toss well to coat evenly.
  6. Transfer everything to a baking dish.
  7. Bake for 40–50 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the chicken is golden brown. For a crispier crust, switch the oven to the broil setting for the last 10 minutes.

SERVING:

Serve with a fresh vegetable salad or pickles. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired.

Enjoy your meal! ❤️
